Step 4 of 6

Tell your business story

This is where buyers decide whether to reach out. Write like you are talking to a smart, curious person who wants to understand what they are buying.

✍️

What makes a great description? Cover what the business does, what makes it different, who the customers are, what comes with the sale, and why it is a good opportunity. You do not need to reveal everything — just enough to get serious buyers asking questions.

Add photos

Listings with photos get 5x more enquiries. Show the space, the team, the product — whatever makes your business come alive.

What photos work best? The exterior, the interior in action, any key equipment, the product or service in use. Avoid blurry or dark shots. The first photo becomes your listing cover image.

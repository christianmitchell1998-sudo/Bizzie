Common questions

How long does my listing stay live?
All private seller listings stay live until your business is sold. There are no renewal fees or expiry dates. You can mark your listing as sold at any time.
Can I upgrade my listing after I have published it?
Yes, you can purchase boost add-ons at any time from your seller dashboard. If you want to upgrade your plan, you will only pay the difference.
Does Bizzie take a commission when the business sells?
No. Bizzie charges a flat listing fee only. We do not take a percentage of the sale price. What you negotiate with the buyer is entirely between you.
Can brokers cancel their subscription at any time?
Yes, broker subscriptions are month to month with no lock-in contract. If you cancel, your listings will remain live until the end of your billing period.
